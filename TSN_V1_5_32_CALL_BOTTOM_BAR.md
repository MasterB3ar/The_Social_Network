# TSN V1.5.32 — Call Bottom Bar + Pop-out Panel

## Changed

- Active calls now live in a fixed bottom bar instead of a small side panel.
- Added a `call-minimized` UI state for active/outgoing calls.
- The call button now says **Pop frem** while minimized and **Tilbage til bjælke** while expanded.
- The active call can be expanded to a full call panel and minimized back to the bottom bar without ending the call.
- The bottom bar keeps timer, status and call controls visible and usable.
- Mobile call controls are now reachable at the bottom of the screen.
